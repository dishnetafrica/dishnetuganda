Starlink accessories

Genuine Starlink mounts, routers, cables and cases — the parts that get your dish onto a wall, a roof, a pole or a vehicle, and your WiFi into the rest of the building. Delivered anywhere in Uganda.

Prices are in UGX, VAT inclusive, and come live from our billing system. Tell us on WhatsApp which item you want — we confirm stock and delivery to your town before you pay.

Mounts, routers, cables and cases

Wall, pivot, ridgeline, pipe, roof-rack and vehicle mounts for the Standard dish and for the Mini; the Router 3 and Router Mini for WiFi across a bigger building; power, cable and travel kit for the Mini. Tell us what you are mounting on and we will send the right part and its price.

Ask for the accessories list

Not sure which mount fits your dish? An item marked Mini fits the Starlink Mini; one marked Standard 4 or 4 X fits the Standard dish. Send us a photo of where you want it mounted and we will tell you exactly what you need.

Need the kit as well?

Every DishNet kit comes with delivery, professional mounting and alignment, router and WiFi setup, and your first month of internet.
