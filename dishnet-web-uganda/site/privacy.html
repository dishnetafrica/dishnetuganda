Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

To provide internet and security services we collect your name, phone number, email, service address, and payment records (mobile money transaction references — never your PIN). Our network equipment also logs technical data such as data usage and device counts, used for billing and troubleshooting.

How we use it

Your information is used to install and operate your service, bill you correctly, respond to support requests, and notify you about your account. We do not sell customer data. We share it only with service providers essential to operating (for example payment processors and Starlink activation), or where the law requires.

Your choices

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete data we're not required to keep — email uganda@dishnetafrica.com. Marketing messages are optional; tell us to stop and we will.

Security & retention

Account data lives in access-controlled systems; payment flows use the mobile money operators' own secure channels. We retain records as long as needed for the service and legal/tax obligations in Uganda, then delete them.

Questions? Contact us — a person will answer.
