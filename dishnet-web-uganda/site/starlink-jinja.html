Starlink in Jinja,
done properly

Jinja's economy runs on two things that both demand serious internet: industry and adventure tourism. Factories and logistics firms along the industrial belt need dependable connectivity for operations, while the rafting camps, kayak schools, and backpacker hostels along the Nile live and die by their WiFi reviews.

DishNet connects both sides of Jinja — Starlink in town and out to the river camps and islands where no cable will ever reach.
