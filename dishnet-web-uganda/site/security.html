A separate DishNet service

Protect what you've built

CCTV and network security are a separate DishNet service — not a Starlink product, and you do not need Starlink to use them. DishNet's engineers supply and install camera systems and firewalls — for homes, shops, schools, banks, hotels and NGOs across Uganda. Your first ICT consultation is free and carries no obligation.

CCTV & Cameras

Camera brands

Every installation includes a site survey, clean cabling, recorder setup, remote viewing on your phone, and staff training.

Hikvision

Full CCTV range — turret, bullet, PTZ, ColorVu night vision, NVRs and storage.

Dahua

Value-to-pro cameras, XVR/NVR recorders, and smart motion detection.

Uniview (UNV)

Reliable IP camera systems with strong low-light performance.

Axis Communications

Enterprise cameras for larger and higher-security sites.

Ubiquiti UniFi Protect

Sleek IP cameras managed from the same UniFi console as your network.

EZVIZ & Imou

Affordable smart home cameras with app viewing and cloud options.

Remote viewing

Power-cut-ready setups

Support afterwards
