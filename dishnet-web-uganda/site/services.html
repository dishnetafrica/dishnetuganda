What DishNet does — and what only Starlink or the regulator decides

DishNet advises on Starlink equipment, plans and how to acquire them; guides you through registering in your own name; offers managed DishNet plans, subject to applicable requirements; plans, installs, configures and tests the equipment where available; and supports you afterwards on WhatsApp, seven days a week.

Starlink or the regulator decides registration approval, KYC verification and activation; eligibility and any restriction on a registration; coverage, capacity and new sign-ups in your area; Starlink's own prices, plans and policies; and licences from the Uganda Communications Commission. DishNet cannot override, bypass or speed up any of these decisions.
