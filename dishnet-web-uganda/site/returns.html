We pay refunds within 14 days, by the method you paid with. Your rights under Ugandan law are not affected.

1. Your rights under the law

If goods are faulty, not as described, or not fit for a purpose you told us about, the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act gives you rights to a repair, replacement, price reduction or refund. Customers who order electronically, for example by WhatsApp, e-mail or online, also have cancellation rights under the Electronic Transactions Act. This page is written to give you at least those rights. We give the change-of-mind right in section 2 to every customer, including those who buy at our office.

2. Changing your mind about equipment

You may return equipment you bought from us within 7 days of the day you received it, for any reason.

  • Return it with all its parts and cables, and the original packaging if you still have it.
  • Do not damage it beyond what was needed to unpack, inspect and try it. If an item comes back damaged, or with parts missing, we may deduct the reasonable cost of the damage or the missing part, as far as the law allows. We tell you the amount and the reason in writing.
  • A Starlink kit that has been activated can still be returned within the period. We take it off the account.
  • We refund the price of the item and the delivery charge you paid for it. You pay only the direct cost of returning it to us. If you prefer, we collect it and deduct that direct cost, which we tell you first.

What change of mind does not cover. These do not affect your rights for faulty items, which are always covered:

  • Services we have already carried out at your request. These are installation work, and internet service already used. If we installed equipment at your request within the 7 days, you can still return the equipment. The installation charge, and the internet service you have used, are not refunded. Your quotation shows these parts separately.
  • Goods made or cut to your specification, such as cable cut to length, or a pole or bracket made for your site.
  • Materials that cannot be returned because they have been built in, such as cable run through your walls.

3. Faulty, damaged or wrong items

Tell us as soon as you notice a problem. If an item was damaged in delivery, is not what you ordered, or is faulty, we arrange collection or exchange at our cost. Within 7 days of delivery or installation you choose a replacement or a full refund. After that, our Warranty Policy and your legal rights apply.

4. Cancelling an order before delivery

You may cancel an order at any time before we dispatch it, and we refund everything you paid. If it is already on its way, you can refuse it on delivery or return it under section 2.

5. Cancelling an installation

The Installation Terms you accept for your job set out these rules:

  • Cancel before the technician has been sent out: no installation or transport charge.
  • Cancel after the technician has been sent out but before work starts: the transport charge shown for your job, if there is one, may be payable.
  • After work has started: you remain responsible for the installation charges and other charges agreed for the job, subject to any cancellation, refund or other right the law does not allow us to exclude.

6. Cancelling a monthly plan

Monthly plans have no fixed term and no cancellation fee. Your service continues until the end of the month you have already paid for. We do not refund the unused part of a month that has started, except for a billing mistake, a service credit under section 11 of our Terms of Service, or where the law requires it. Whole months paid in advance and not yet started are refunded. Equipment that belongs to us must be returned (section 9 of the Terms of Service). Flex packages and other packages with a minimum term follow their own written agreement.

7. How refunds are paid

  • We pay within 14 days of receiving the returned item, or of agreeing the refund where nothing needs to be returned. That is never later than 30 days.
  • We refund the full amount, including VAT, and issue a credit note for it.
  • Refunds go back by the method you paid with:
    • Airtel Money refunds go to the number that paid.
    • Bank refunds go to an account in the payer's name, or to an account you give us in writing.
    • Cash payments are refunded by Airtel Money or bank transfer, or in cash at our office against your receipt and ID.
  • We pay any charge for sending the refund. We do not charge a refund or restocking fee.

8. How to return something

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp at +256 705 993 348, by e-mail at info@dishnetafrica.com, or at our office. Give your name and receipt or invoice number. We give you a return reference.
  2. Bring the item to our office, or send it the way we agree. Upcountry we use a parcel service or collect it.
  3. We check the item within 2 working days of receiving it and tell you the result.
  4. If we cannot accept a return, we explain why in writing and send the item back to you at our cost. You can take the matter further through our Complaints process.
