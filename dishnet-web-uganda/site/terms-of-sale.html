  • We deliver anywhere in Uganda. Your quotation gives the delivery charge, if there is one. We tell you the expected delivery time when you order.

  • Equipment becomes yours when you have paid for it in full.
  • From the time it is delivered to you, or to someone you ask us to deliver it to, you are responsible for keeping it safe.
  • A kit on a package that is paid over time (such as a Flex package) is governed by the written agreement you sign for it, and by the Hire Purchase Act where that Act applies.

7. Starlink kits

  • The kits we sell are genuine Starlink equipment for use in Uganda.
  • If you buy a kit for a Starlink account in your own name, we help you order, subscribe and activate it.
  • On a DishNet plan, our managed service, we activate your kit for you, and your contract for the service is with DishNet, not with Starlink. The Starlink account that serves your kit is registered and managed by DishNet on your behalf, and you can ask us to transfer it to you (see section 3 of our Terms of Service). Where the law requires it, your kit and account details are registered with the Uganda Communications Commission.

If Starlink will not activate a kit you bought from us, for a reason that is not your fault, we replace it with one that Starlink activates or refund everything you paid for it, as you choose.

It does not cover damage caused by an installation we did not carry out.
