About us

A local ICT partner that helps you decide, then delivers

DishNet Africa has operated since 2013, starting in South Sudan, where connecting people was hardest.

DishNet Africa Limited is an independent Ugandan company — an IT solutions provider and Starlink installer, registered in Uganda. DishNet Africa has operated since 2013: it began in South Sudan, building internet where there were no cables, no towers and often no grid power.

In Uganda, DishNet brings that experience to homes, businesses, NGOs, schools and institutions. DishNet serves more than 40 towns, from our office in Kampala to installations upcountry — because a trading centre in Kisoro deserves the same internet as an office tower in Kampala.

Our approach starts with your requirements, not a product: who will use the connection, where, on what power, and what it has to support. We explain your Starlink options in plain language — registering with Starlink in your own name, with our guidance, or a managed DishNet plan — then plan the installation and stay with you afterwards. A managed DishNet plan is DishNet's own service: your contract is with DishNet, and the Starlink account that serves your kit is registered and managed by DishNet on your behalf. If Starlink is not the right fit for you, we will tell you honestly. Your first ICT consultation is free and carries no obligation.

What makes us different is what happens after installation. WhatsApp support is available seven days a week, billing works with Airtel Money, and when hardware needs attention we arrange a technician visit in the towns we serve, from Ntinda to Nebbi.

Independent of Starlink. DishNet is not part of Starlink or SpaceX. It is not an agent, partner, franchisee, affiliate or representative of Starlink or SpaceX. Starlink is a trademark of its owner. Starlink controls service availability, eligibility, registration approval, KYC verification and activation. Managed DishNet plans are DishNet's own service, not Starlink products.
