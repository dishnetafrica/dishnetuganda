About us

Connecting East Africa since 2013

DishNet Africa Ltd started by connecting the hardest places first — and never stopped.

DishNet Africa began in South Sudan, building internet where there were no cables, no towers, and often no grid power. Over a decade we became an official Starlink reseller and installer, built fiber networks in major cities, and connected thousands of homes, businesses, NGOs, and government offices.

Uganda is the natural next chapter. The same team, the same engineering standards, and the same belief drive our work here: that a trading centre in Kisoro deserves the same internet as an office tower in Kampala. We install Starlink anywhere in the country, run fiber in the major cities, help local entrepreneurs build WiFi businesses, and secure premises with professional CCTV and firewalls.

What makes us different is what happens after installation. Our support runs on WhatsApp in the languages our customers speak, billing works with MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, and our technicians actually come when something needs fixing — whether that's in Ntinda or Nebbi.
