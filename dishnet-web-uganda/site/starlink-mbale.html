Internet in Mbale,
done properly

From the slopes of Mt. Elgon, Bugisu's coffee moves through Mbale to the world — and the exporters, cooperatives, and banks that move it need internet that doesn't fail during a contract call. The city's schools and hospitals carry the same dependence.

DishNet serves Mbale with fiber in the city, Starlink up the mountain and across the districts, and WiFi zones for the arcades and student areas. Highland weather and steep terrain don't bother a dish that talks straight to the sky.
