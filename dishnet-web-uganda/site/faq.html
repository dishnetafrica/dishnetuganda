Yes — Starlink works everywhere in Uganda with a clear view of the sky, from Kampala to the most remote village. We deliver and install kits in all four regions. Check the coverage page for towns where we also offer fiber and hotspot zones.
Pricing depends on the kit (Mini, Standard, or High Performance) and current Starlink service plans. Message us on WhatsApp for today's prices — we'll give you a full quote covering the kit, delivery to your town, professional installation, and the monthly subscription.
In Kampala and major cities, usually 1–3 working days after payment. For upcountry towns we schedule delivery and installation together, typically within a week. The installation itself takes 2–4 hours.
Yes. We accept MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money for equipment, installation, and monthly bills. You can also pay directly inside the DishNet app or through the customer portal.
