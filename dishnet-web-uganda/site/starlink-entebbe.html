Starlink in Entebbe,
done properly

Between the airport, the UN and NGO offices, lakeside hotels, and a fast-growing residential population, Entebbe's demand for reliable internet outgrew its infrastructure years ago. Guests expect hotel WiFi that actually works, and organisations here can't afford connections that drop during a video call with headquarters.

DishNet serves Entebbe with Starlink for homes, offices, lakeside properties and compounds, and managed guest WiFi for hotels and guesthouses.
