Questions

Before you get connected

Quick answers to the questions customers ask most.

View all FAQs →
Can DishNet install Starlink at my home?

Yes. Tell us your location and we can guide you through the available equipment, plan and installation options.

How do I choose the right plan?

It depends on your location, number of users and how you use the connection. Home plans give unlimited data; Business plans add priority data and a public IP. Tell us your town and how many people will use it, and we'll say which fits.

Do you provide support after installation?

Yes. Use the customer portal, WhatsApp +256 705 993 348 (we answer every day), call the same number, or email info@dishnetafrica.com.

Do you serve customers outside Kampala?

Yes. We deliver and install anywhere in Uganda where the dish can see the sky, and we check your address before you pay. Look for your town on the coverage page; if it isn't there, message us on WhatsApp.
