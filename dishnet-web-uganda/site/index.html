About DishNet

A local ICT partner that helps you decide, then delivers

DishNet Africa Limited is an independent Ugandan company — an IT solutions provider and Starlink installer, registered in Uganda. DishNet Africa has operated since 2013: it began in South Sudan, building internet where there were no cables, no towers and often no grid power.

In Uganda, DishNet brings that experience to homes, businesses, NGOs, schools and institutions. DishNet serves more than 40 towns, from our office in Kampala to installations upcountry.

Our approach starts with your requirements, not a product: who will use the connection, where, on what power, and what it has to support. We explain your Starlink options in plain language, plan the installation, and stay with you afterwards — and if Starlink is not the right fit for you, we will tell you honestly.

More questions answered

How can DishNet help me acquire Starlink?

We start with your free consultation: where and how you will use the connection, how many people, and on what power. We then help you choose the kit and plan, explain how to register Starlink in your own name with our guidance, and plan the installation with a written quotation.

Starlink controls registration approval, KYC verification and activation.

Can I register Starlink in my own name?

Yes. We help you understand and navigate Starlink's registration: what it asks for, how to prepare the information, and how to complete each step correctly. The Starlink account is then yours, under Starlink's own terms, and we can still install and support the equipment.

Starlink controls registration approval, KYC verification and activation. We cannot promise approval, or speed it up.

What if my own-name registration is restricted?

Only Starlink can change its decision. We can help you understand the message you received, check whether information was missing or entered incorrectly, and help you contact Starlink's support.

Each case is reviewed individually, subject to Starlink's terms, requirements and approvals. We will never help anyone bypass verification, registration restrictions or eligibility checks.

Which Starlink kit suits my requirements?

It depends on where you will use it, how many people will connect, and your power. Mini is compact and portable; Standard suits homes, offices and other fixed sites; High Performance is for organisations that cannot afford downtime. Try the kit guide for a starting point, then confirm it in your free consultation.

Is Starlink available at my location?

Starlink can be used where the dish has a clear view of the sky. Where Starlink's network is busy it can limit new sign-ups, so we check your address before you pay. Coverage and capacity are Starlink's: we cannot promise service where Starlink does not offer it.

How do I request installation or technical support?

A new installation: book your free consultation, and we plan the installation with you and confirm it in a written quotation. In Kampala and major cities we usually install within 1–3 working days of payment; upcountry, we schedule delivery and installation together, typically within a week. The installation itself usually takes 2–4 hours.

Support for an existing service: message us on WhatsApp — WhatsApp support is available seven days a week — with your name and what is happening. We fix what we can remotely first, and arrange a technician visit where needed and available. You can also check invoices and payments in the customer portal.

How do I arrange my free consultation?

Message us on WhatsApp, call +256 705 993 348, email info@dishnetafrica.com, or use the form on this page, which prepares a WhatsApp message for you to send. Your first ICT consultation is free and carries no obligation.

Businesses and NGOs do not need one before buying, but we recommend it: it covers your users, sites, power, budget and how much downtime you can accept, and gives you a written quotation for your procurement process.

Do I need Starlink for DishNet's CCTV or network security?

No. CCTV and network security are a separate DishNet service. They are not Starlink products, and you do not need Starlink to use them. See CCTV and network security.
