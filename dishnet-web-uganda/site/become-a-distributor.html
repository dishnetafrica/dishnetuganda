Why partner with DishNet

Built around a local team

🤝 One local team

Sales, installation, billing and technical support are coordinated through DishNet's Uganda operation.

🎓 Training provided

Partners receive structured training suited to their model before going live. Technical installation is a separate certification.

🛠️ Technical backup

Partners escalate to authorised DishNet technicians. Complex installations can be handled jointly.

📦 Genuine hardware

Where a model includes hardware, it is genuine, supported equipment — handled under agreed terms.

🧭 Clear processes

Orders, onboarding, support and (where relevant) stock follow defined DishNet procedures.

🌍 Reach across Uganda

DishNet installs and supports across four regions and 40+ towns, and keeps growing.
