Internet in Soroti,
done properly

Soroti anchors Teso — a city known for its flying school, its citrus, and its role as the gateway between eastern and northern Uganda. Businesses and institutions here have long paid too much for too little internet.

DishNet brings Starlink to Soroti city and every district around it, WiFi zones to the commercial centre and student areas, and dependable connections for the schools, hospital, and aviation community.
