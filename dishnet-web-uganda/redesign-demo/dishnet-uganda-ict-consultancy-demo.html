About DishNet

A local ICT partner that helps you decide, then delivers

DishNet Africa Limited is an independent Ugandan company — an IT solutions provider and Starlink installer, registered in Uganda. We began in South Sudan in 2013, building internet where there were no cables, no towers and often no grid power.

In Uganda, the same team brings that experience to homes, businesses, NGOs, schools and institutions, from our office in Kampala to installations upcountry.

Our approach starts with your requirements, not a product: who will use the connection, where, on what power, and what it has to support. We help you compare your options in plain language, plan the deployment, and stay with you after installation — and if Starlink is not the right fit, we say so.

Acquiring Starlink

Two ways to get Starlink with DishNet

We will explain both before you decide.

Option 1

Buy in your own name, with our help

We advise you on the kit and plan, help you order and subscribe, and install and support it for you.

  • The Starlink account is yours, under Starlink's own terms
  • Guidance through ordering and verification
  • Installation, Wi-Fi setup and local support from DishNet
Option 2

A DishNet plan — our managed service

Your contract for the service is with DishNet, not with Starlink, and the Starlink account that serves your kit is registered and managed by DishNet on your behalf.

  • One local bill, payable by Airtel Money, bank transfer or cash
  • Installation, warranty handling and support from one team
  • You can ask us to transfer the account to you, under Starlink's rules

Any minimum period, fee and processing time for an account transfer is confirmed to you in writing first. See our Terms of Service.

All FAQs on the live site

How can I acquire Starlink in Uganda?

With DishNet there are two ways. You can buy in your own name: we advise you on the kit and plan, help you order and subscribe, and install and support it — the Starlink account is then yours, under Starlink's own terms. Or you can choose a DishNet plan, our managed service, where the Starlink account is registered and managed by DishNet on your behalf.

A consultation helps you decide which suits you. Either way, Starlink decides eligibility and approval.

Which Starlink kit is suitable for my requirements?

It depends on where you will use it, how many people will connect, and your power. Mini is compact and portable; Standard suits homes, offices and other fixed sites; High Performance is for organisations that cannot afford downtime. Try the kit guide for a starting point, then let an adviser confirm it.

Can DishNet help me with registration and verification?

Yes — with guidance. We explain what Starlink's ordering and verification steps ask for, help you prepare the information and complete the steps correctly. Starlink makes every registration, verification and approval decision: we cannot guarantee the outcome, or speed it up.

What if my account or registration is restricted?

Only Starlink can change its decision. We can help you understand the message you received, check whether information was missing or entered incorrectly, and help you contact Starlink's support. If Starlink is still not available to you, we will talk you through other ways to get connected.

We never register a service in someone else's name to get around Starlink's checks.

Can DishNet help with installation and Wi-Fi configuration?

Yes. Our technicians check the site, mount and align the dish, run the cabling, set up your router and Wi-Fi, test it, and show you how it works. The installation itself usually takes 2–4 hours. In Kampala and major cities we usually install within 1–3 working days of payment; upcountry, we schedule delivery and installation together, typically within a week.

Is Starlink available at my location?

Starlink can be used anywhere in Uganda where the dish has a clear view of the sky. Where Starlink's network is busy it can limit new sign-ups, so we check your address before you pay. Coverage and capacity are Starlink's: we cannot promise service where Starlink does not offer it. See our coverage page.

Do businesses and NGOs need a consultation before purchasing?

It is not required, but we recommend it. A short consultation covers your users, sites, power, budget and how much downtime you can accept — so you buy the right equipment once — and gives you a written quotation for your procurement process.

Can DishNet recommend alternatives if Starlink is unsuitable?

Yes. If Starlink is not the right fit — because of cost, location, power or how you will use it — we will tell you, and talk you through the other options that may be available where you are.
