3. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiries, prepare quotations and process orders.
  • To deliver, install, activate and support your equipment and service, including sending your activation details to Starlink.
  • To bill you, record payments, and keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.
  • To send service messages about bills, payments, faults, installation visits and changes to your service.
  • To meet legal duties, for example identity or equipment registration that the law or the Uganda Communications Commission requires, and lawful requests from authorities.
  • To keep our systems and customers safe, prevent fraud, and deal with complaints and legal claims.
  • To improve our service, using the information in a summarised form wherever we can.
  • To follow up on an enquiry you made, to send offers and news if you have agreed, and to send one message inviting you back after a service ends (section 11).

We use your information where it is needed to carry out our agreement with you, to comply with the law, or with your consent. Where the law requires your consent, we ask for it, and you may withdraw it at any time. We treat payment information as sensitive financial information, and use it only to take and record your payments and refunds, to prevent fraud and to keep the records tax law requires.

4. What you must give us, and what is optional

To provide a service we need your name, a phone number, the installation address and, where the law or activation requires it, proof of identity. Without these we cannot provide the service. An e-mail address is optional but helpful. Agreeing to marketing is always optional.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only as follows, and only what each one needs:

  • Starlink (SpaceX), to activate and run Starlink service on your equipment. Starlink uses that information under its own Privacy Policy, published at starlink.com/legal.
  • Payment providers and banks, such as Airtel Money, to process and confirm payments and refunds.
  • Uganda Revenue Authority, for the tax invoices, receipts and records the law requires.
  • The Uganda Communications Commission and other authorities, where the law requires it, for example for registration of equipment or users, or under a court order or lawful request.
  • Companies that provide services to us, under contract and only to carry out our instructions. These include hosting, storage and back-up, e-mail, and artificial-intelligence services that help us answer messages (section 7).
  • WhatsApp (Meta), which carries the messages we exchange with you on WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Our technicians, delivery partners and authorised DishNet partners who deliver, install or support your service.
  • Professional advisers, such as auditors and lawyers, and a buyer of our business if it is ever sold. They must keep your information confidential.

6. Information stored or used outside Uganda

Some of the companies above store or process information outside Uganda. Examples: Starlink, WhatsApp, e-mail and cloud services, AI services, and the servers that host our customer systems. We transfer personal information outside Uganda only where the law allows it: where the country that receives it protects personal information at least as well as Ugandan law, or where you have agreed to the transfer. When you order a Starlink service, we ask you to agree to your activation details being shared with Starlink.

7. Automated replies and AI

Some replies on WhatsApp and in our website chat are written by an automated assistant that uses artificial-intelligence services. You can ask for a person at any time. Our billing system pauses a service automatically at the end of an unpaid period, as our Terms of Service describe; you can ask a person to review it at any time. Apart from that automatic pause, we do not use automated decisions to refuse you a service or change your price without a person involved.

8. How long we keep it

  • Customer, billing, installation and support records: while your account is open, and then for six years, the period within which a legal claim about them can usually be brought, or longer if tax law requires it.
  • Enquiries that did not lead to an order: up to two years after our last contact.
  • Your marketing choices: as long as needed to respect them, including a record that you opted out.

When we no longer need information, we delete it, destroy it or remove the details that identify you.

9. Security

We protect personal information with access controls, encrypted connections, staff confidentiality and limited access on a need-to-know basis. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your information at risk, we notify the regulator as the law requires, and we tell you where we need to.

10. Your rights

You may ask us:

  • whether we hold information about you, what it is, and who it has been shared with, and for a copy of it;
  • to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • to delete information we no longer need, or that we should not hold;
  • to stop using your information in a way that causes, or is likely to cause, you unwarranted damage or distress;
  • to stop sending you marketing, at any time;
  • to withdraw a consent you have given.

Contact us using the details in section 1. We may ask you to prove your identity, so that we do not give your information to someone else. We reply within 14 days. Some records we must keep by law, for example tax records, even if you ask us to delete them. If you do, we tell you which and why.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (pdpo.go.ug).

11. Marketing messages

We send offers and news only if you have agreed to receive them, with two exceptions: if you ask us about a product or service, we may follow up on that enquiry; and when a service ends, we may send you one message inviting you back. Reply STOP on WhatsApp, or tell us any other way, and we stop. You will still receive service messages about your bills, payments, installation and faults.

12. This website and our chat

This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. It loads its fonts from Google's font service, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Our website chat keeps your last 20 messages in your own browser so that you can continue later; clearing your browser's site data removes that copy. Everything you type is also sent to our systems, where our team can read it, together with any name, number or e-mail you leave. It is handled like any other message to us, including by our automated assistant (section 7). We delete website chats, and the details left in them, after 90 days. We keep your IP address for up to two days to prevent abuse of the chat.

13. Children, cameras and changes

  • Children. Our services are for adults; accounts are opened by people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information about children, except where a parent or guardian gives it to us.
  • WiFi zones. If you connect at a WiFi zone we manage, we process the technical details needed to connect your device, such as its network identifier and the times it was connected. WiFi zones are not meant for children without a parent's or guardian's agreement.
  • Cameras we install for you. You decide what your cameras record and how the recordings are used, and you are responsible for using them lawfully. We access recordings only as needed to install, maintain or repair your system, and only on your instructions.
  • Changes. We update this policy when our practices or the law change, and show the date at the top. If a change affects you significantly, we tell you.
