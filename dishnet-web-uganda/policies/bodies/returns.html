1. Your rights under the law

If goods are faulty, not as described, or not fit for a purpose you told us about, the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act gives you rights to a repair, replacement, price reduction or refund. Customers who order electronically, for example by WhatsApp, e-mail or online, also have cancellation rights under the Electronic Transactions Act. This page is written to give you at least those rights. Where the law gives you more, the law applies. We give the change-of-mind right in section 2 to every customer, including those who buy at our office.
