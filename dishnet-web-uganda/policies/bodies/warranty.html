1. Your rights under the law come first

Under the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act and other laws of Uganda, what we sell must match its description, be of satisfactory quality (which includes being durable) and be fit for any purpose you told us about. Our services, including installation, must be carried out with reasonable care and skill. If something we sold or did falls short of that, you may be entitled to a repair, a replacement, a price reduction or a refund. That applies even after a warranty period has ended. Nothing on this page reduces those rights.

The warranty covers faults in the equipment and in our work. It does not cover:

  • physical damage, drops, crushing, water damage to indoor units, fire, or damage by animals, for example rodents chewing cables;
  • problems caused by your power supply: unstable mains, generators or inverters without suitable protection, or the wrong power adapter;
  • equipment opened, modified, repainted or repaired by anyone other than us or the manufacturer, or loaded with firmware that did not come from the manufacturer;
  • equipment moved or re-installed by someone else, or installation work we did not do;
  • use in motion, away from the installation address or outside Uganda on a plan that does not allow it;
  • obstructions that appear after installation, such as new buildings or growing trees;

  • settings changed by you or someone else;
  • interruptions to the Starlink network or another network. These are service matters, covered in our Terms of Service.
