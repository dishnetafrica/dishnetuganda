This page summarises the Installation Terms. The version you accept for your job is the one that applies to it, and a later version does not change it.

1. You approve the job before we start

We send you a link to a secure DishNet page, by WhatsApp or e-mail. It shows your Installation Job: the job number, the installation address, the service and equipment, the charges and, where one has been set, the date. You read the Installation Terms there and accept them. We do not start work until your acceptance is recorded. The record keeps the reference, the time, the version of the terms and the details shown to you. That is the evidence of what you approved.

2. What a standard installation includes

  • Choosing a position for the dish with you, with a clear view of the sky, and mounting it.
  • Routing the cable, and placing and connecting the router and power supply.
  • Activating the service and checking that it works.
  • Setting up your WiFi and showing you the app.

Work that is not described in your Installation Job is not included.

3. Extra materials and work

Some sites need more, such as longer cable, a pole, extra brackets, trenching, conduit or electrical work. If so, the technician explains what is needed and what it costs before doing it, and goes ahead only with your agreement. You may say no. We then complete as much as the approved work allows, or reschedule.

4. What we need from you

  • Safe access at the agreed time to the roof, walls, rooms and anywhere else the equipment or cable will go.
  • Permission from the owner or landlord if the property is rented or shared.
  • A working power point where the router will be, and power at the site.
  • Correct contact and address details. Someone should be present to agree the dish position and cable route.

5. Scheduling and visits

Either of us may ask to reschedule before the visit, and we confirm any new date. If nobody can give access at the agreed time, the visit may need to be rescheduled. The transport charge shown for your job, if there is one, may then apply.

6. Cancelling

  • Before the technician is sent out: no charge.
  • After the technician is sent out but before work starts: the transport charge shown for your job, if there is one, may apply.
  • After work has started: the agreed charges remain payable, subject to any cancellation, refund or other right the law does not allow us to exclude.

7. Completion and sign-off

The installation is complete when the equipment is installed as described and the service is confirmed working. If part of the work cannot be finished on site, it is complete when the rest is done and what remains is recorded with you. The technician may ask you to confirm completion. Your confirmation records that you received the installation. It is separate from your approval of the job, and it does not take away any of your rights.

8. Safety, care and your property

Our technicians work safely and follow the manufacturer's installation guidance. We are responsible for damage our technician causes by negligence. As far as the law allows, we are not responsible for problems that existed at the site before the installation, for results of instructions you gave, or for work done by others. We photograph the finished installation and record the position of the dish, to support warranty and future service visits.

9. Workmanship warranty

For {{WORKMANSHIP}} after completion, we fix free of charge any fault caused by our own installation work. See our Warranty Policy.

10. If you install it yourself

If you buy equipment without installation, you or your installer are responsible for installing it safely. The equipment warranty still applies. Damage caused by an installation we did not carry out is not covered.
