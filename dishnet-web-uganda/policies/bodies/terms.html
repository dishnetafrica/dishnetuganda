3. Our service and what it depends on

We tell you the typical speeds for your plan before you buy, but speeds are not guaranteed. Satellite and wireless performance changes with weather, obstructions such as trees and buildings, the number of people online at busy times, and the network itself. Some plans are given lower priority on the network at busy times; we tell you before you choose one.

Your service needs the equipment installed with a clear view of the sky (for Starlink) and a working power supply. It also needs the Starlink network or our fiber network to be available. We will always be straight with you about what to expect at your site.
