3. Our service and what it depends on

On a DishNet plan, your contract for the service is with DishNet, not with Starlink. The Starlink account that serves your kit is registered and managed by DishNet on your behalf. You can ask us to transfer it to you; any minimum period, fee and processing time are confirmed to you in writing before a transfer, and transfers follow Starlink's own rules.

We tell you the typical speeds for your plan before you buy, but speeds are not guaranteed. Satellite and wireless performance changes with weather, obstructions such as trees and buildings, the number of people online at busy times, and the network itself. Some plans are given lower priority on the network at busy times; we tell you before you choose one.

Your service needs the equipment installed with a clear view of the sky (for Starlink) and a working power supply. It also needs the Starlink network or our fiber network to be available. We will always be straight with you about what to expect at your site.

7. Unpaid bills and suspension

Plans are paid in advance. We send you a reminder before each month is due. Service pauses automatically at the end of an unpaid period and resumes when your payment is applied to your account. Our billing system does this automatically; you can ask a person to review it at any time.

We restore the service once the payment is applied to your account. That is usually the same working day (Monday to Friday, except public holidays in Uganda); if you paid by mobile money, send us the transaction ID.

A pause for an unpaid bill does not end your plan. If the bill is still unpaid while the service is paused, we may end the plan (section 16), but only after telling you in writing that we will and giving you a date to pay by. Equipment that belongs to us must then be returned (section 9), except a kit on a Flex package, which follows its own agreement and the law.
